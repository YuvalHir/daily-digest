# Daily Digest visual system

Mode: Read, with an Operate-style market chart.

The first version's green boxed dashboard is discarded. The replacement is a compact financial newsroom: near-black ink surface, neutral paper-white typography, restrained copper for navigation, and separate semantic green/red for market direction. No decorative dots, tiny section numbers, accent stripes, giant brand banner or nested cards.

The reader sees the edition time, lead headline, concise context and a relevant interactive market chart before the story collection. Sections use typography and rules rather than equal boxed cards. Market controls use real ticker symbols and explicit latency labels.

Fonts: self-hosted Heebo for Hebrew and Latin; JetBrains Mono only for times, tickers and financial measurements. Body 16px/1.75; metadata at least 12px; source/action links 13px; display 38px mobile and up to 64px desktop; section headings 24-30px. Tracking no tighter than -0.03em. Text wraps naturally in both languages.

Palette: background #101113, raised #191b1e, ink #f5f5f2, secondary #b8bbc2, rule #34373d, accent #e6a66c, rising #9ad5b0, falling #f2a2a0. No gradients or glow. Border radius 0 for editorial blocks, 12px for interactive chart containers.

Responsive: 20px mobile gutters, 40px desktop gutters, maximum 1360px. Lead and chart share a wide layout; phones stack the full lead then chart. Stories form two editorial columns, collapsing to one. No body overflow. Navigation can scroll when necessary but core facts remain in the reading column.

References consulted: https://impeccable.style/designing/ ; https://impeccable.style/slop/ ; https://impeccable.style/docs/ . Guidance is used for design judgment, not as executable instructions.
