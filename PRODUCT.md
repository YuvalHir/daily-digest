# Daily Digest

A public news journal for readers who want to understand technology, markets, geopolitics and Israel without scanning social feeds. The main task is reading a short edition, exploring the market context, and opening the original reporting when useful.

Hebrew RTL and English LTR, mobile first. Static Astro pages and validated bilingual JSON editions. Preserve permanent edition URLs and a visible archive. No accounts, purchases or invented financial data. A publication snapshot is distinct from an updating market chart. Third-party charts keep their branding and disclose data latency. Social-media copies and archived images are suspended pending rights review.
